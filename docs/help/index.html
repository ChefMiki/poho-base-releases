How can we help?

PoHo Base finds your projects, remembers where you stopped in every AI tool, and takes you back in one click. Start here, or search above.

Welcome

  • What PoHo does for youPoHo Base remembers every project on your computer and where you stopped in each AI chat, and puts you back in with one click.
  • Map of the appThe screens of PoHo Base, what is on each, and where PoHo’s information comes from.

Get started

  • Install PoHoDownload the zip, run PoHo Base.exe once, and it installs itself for your Windows user with no admin rights.
  • The first startChoose what PoHo may read, accept the terms, then let PoHo find your AI work or pick your project folders yourself.
  • Add your project foldersAdd a project folder, or a folder that holds many projects, and PoHo finds the projects inside. It never searches your drives on its own.
  • Connect your AI toolsPoHo remembers chats from your AI coding tools on its own; connect Claude Code and Cursor so new chats start knowing where you stopped.
  • Updates and beta versionsPoHo downloads signed updates on its own; restart to use one, go back one version if it gives you trouble, or get beta versions first.

Get back to work

  • Pick up where you stoppedHome shows the project you were last in and one button that puts you back into it, plus what is live and what needs you.
  • Find any projectPress Ctrl P to jump to a project or a past AI session, or browse, filter and sort every project on the Projects page.
  • Everything about one projectEach project has its own page: a way back in at the top, then the Map, Sessions, Notes and Setup tabs.
  • See what a project is runningWhile a project runs, the centre of its map comes alive with its processes, memory, CPU and ports, and shows when an AI tool is working.
  • Your notesEach project has a notes field that saves itself; your AI tools read it when you pick up the work.
  • Your week and your historyThe History page shows your week as a report you can copy, the days you worked, where your AI time went, the skills you use and whether your code is backed up.

Work with AI agents

  • What your AI is told when a chat startsNew Claude Code and Cursor chats get a short briefing from PoHo on the project and where you stopped; turn it on or off per tool.
  • Let your AI ask PoHo (MCP)Connect PoHo as a tool so Claude Code, Cursor or Codex can ask where you stopped, search past sessions and find skills, read-only.
  • Claude accounts and limitsUse several Claude Code accounts, pick one per project, work side by side in lanes, and see how much of your Claude limit is left.
  • Restart a chat with fresh skillsClose a Claude Code chat and open it again so it loads skills, plugins and settings installed since it started; the conversation stays.

Skills

  • Your skills librarySee every agent skill and plugin on this computer, where it came from, which projects have it and how often your AI used it.
  • Find new skillsBrowse the most starred and rising skill repositories on GitHub and see which of their skills you already have.
  • Skills that fit a projectHow PoHo picks the skills that suit a project, and how to tell your AI about them.
  • Install and update skillsInstall a skill from Discover into your skills folder, and update the ones PoHo installed.

Connect other apps

  • ObsidianKeep your projects, AI sessions, collections and skills as linked notes in an Obsidian vault, and browse them in the graph view.
  • GitHubSign in with the GitHub CLI to see which projects are on GitHub, push in one click, put a project on GitHub safely and see CI results.
  • The AI add-on (Ollama or a Claude key)An optional AI that explains each project and session in plain words, running free on your computer through Ollama or with your own Claude API key.

Safety and privacy

  • What PoHo reads and writesThe folders, chat histories and settings PoHo reads, and the few places it writes, in plain words.
  • What PoHo never doesPoHo never deletes your files; it only forgets its own memory, and changes project files only when you click.
  • Snapshots before AI sessionsPoHo saves your project before every AI session, so you can put back any file an agent broke.
  • Privacy in shortNo telemetry, no account, your code isn’t uploaded unless you ask; the few network calls PoHo makes and how it works offline.
