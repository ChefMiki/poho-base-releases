Get back to work

Find any project

Press Ctrl P to jump to a project or a past AI session, or browse, filter and sort every project on the Projects page.

Press Ctrl P anywhere in PoHo and type a few letters: the project you mean comes up, and Enter takes you back into it. To browse instead, open Projects in the top bar.

Jump to a project

  1. Press Ctrl P, or click Jump to a project… in the top bar.
  2. Type part of a project’s name, or something you once asked an AI.
  3. Pick a result with the arrow keys.
  4. Press Enter.
KeyOn a projectOn an AI session
EnterContinue last AI session when it can be reopened, otherwise Open in your editorContinue this session
Shift+EnterOpen in your editor
Ctrl+EnterDetails (the project’s page)Open its project
EscCloseClose

The same box also opens from outside PoHo with Alt+Space. You can change or turn off that shortcut in Settings.

Search inside conversations

As you type, PoHo also lists AI sessions whose title, summary or requests match. To look through the full text of your conversations, choose the last row, Search inside all conversations for “…”. It takes a few seconds and reads Claude Code conversations only. Search runs on your computer; nothing is sent anywhere.

The Projects page

At the top, tabs choose what you see:

  • All: every project, with the count.
  • Favorites: the projects you starred.
  • Skills: the skills and plugins your projects use. See Your skills library.
  • Running and Archived appear only when a dev server started with Run is running, or a project is archived.

Collections

Next to the tabs, each collection is a chip with its count. Click one to show only its projects, and click it again to show all.

  • New collection: type a name and press Enter.
  • Sort automatically: PoHo sorts your projects into collections and stages, with AI when the AI add-on is on, otherwise from the files. Stages and collections you set by hand stay as they are.
  • The × on a chip deletes the collection. Its projects stay.

Add a project to a collection on the project’s page, under its name.

Filter, sort and layout

  • Filter projects… matches names, descriptions, paths, notes, branches and stack. Enter opens the first result in your editor; Esc clears the field.
  • Filter narrows by stage or stack. Clear filters removes them.
  • The sort button orders by Last activity, Name, Last AI chat or Uncommitted changes. Favorites always come first.
  • List and Cards switch between rows and picture cards. PoHo remembers your choice.

Click a project to open its page. Every other action, like Open a terminal here or Remove from PoHo…, is in its ⋯ menu. Removing only hides the project in PoHo; your files stay.
