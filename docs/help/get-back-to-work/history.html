Get back to work

Your week and your history

The History page shows your week as a report you can copy, the days you worked, where your AI time went, the skills you use and whether your code is backed up.

Open History in the top bar to see what you did this week, and where your time with AI went across all projects. Everything on the page comes from your AI chats and commits on this computer. The one outside question: when the GitHub CLI is signed in, PoHo asks GitHub whether your repositories are public or private.

This week

The first card sums up the week:

  • the time you spent with AI, as the big number,
  • Commits, Projects and AI chats,
  • one bar per project, with the first thing done, a commit message or a chat title under it.

Switch between This week and Last week. On Mondays the page opens on last week. Click a project’s bar to open the project.

Copy as report copies the week as plain text, ready to paste into a stand-up, a time sheet or a message.

Days with work

One square per day, from your first active week on. The darker the square, the more AI time and commits that day. Point at a square to see the date, the hours with AI, the commits and the projects. Above the squares: your active days, hours with AI and commits in total.

This month by project

Your top eight projects this month by AI time, with the time written at the end of each bar. Click one to open it.

Skills and tools you use

Every skill and MCP tool your AI sessions used, all time, most used first: what kind it is, in how many projects, when it was last used and how many times. Show all lists the rest. Skills library opens the full list of skills on this computer; see Your skills library.

Is your code backed up?

One bar shows your projects by where their code lives: Private on GitHub, Public on GitHub, Online, visibility unknown, Only on this computer and No git, each with a count and share. Projects that exist only here are named under Only here:. You can put one on GitHub from its ⋯ menu.

AI use by month

The last six months: the tokens used each month, with the hours under each month. Point at a month to see the number of chats and an estimate of what it would cost at API prices. That is an estimate, not a bill.

Note: PoHo reads your commits with git log and your AI sessions from the files your AI tools save. It only reads; it never changes your repositories. Archived projects are left out.
