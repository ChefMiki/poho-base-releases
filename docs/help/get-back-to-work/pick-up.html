Get back to work

Pick up where you stopped

Home shows the project you were last in and one button that puts you back into it, plus what is live and what needs you.

Open PoHo and Home shows the project you were last working in, with a sentence on where you stopped. Press Enter, or click the dark button, to get straight back in.

The big card

The large card at the top is the project in progress you touched last. Projects with the stage Done or Reference never show here. On the card you see:

  • when you were last active, and how many days you were away if it has been a while,
  • one or two sentences on where you stopped,
  • the last AI session and its title, files not committed, and the next step when PoHo knows one.

Click the project’s name to open its page.

The dark button

The button does the best thing it can, in this order:

  1. Continue in … reopens the exact session where it ended, in your editor or a terminal.
  2. Pick up with … starts an AI tool on the project with notes on where the work stopped, when the last session can’t be reopened.
  3. Open in … opens the project in your editor, when there is no AI tool to use.

While the project runs, Open localhost:… sits next to it; otherwise Open site, when the project has a public site.

More ways

More ways holds every other way back in:

  • Restart this chat, to load skills installed since the session started,
  • Pick up with notes on where you stopped: every AI tool you have installed, and Copy the notes,
  • Open in: Cursor, VS Code, Terminal or File Explorer,
  • Open project, the project’s own page.

The rest of Home

  • Live now (right column) appears only while an AI tool is working, waiting for you, or just finished. Waiting comes first. Click a row to open that project.
  • Needs you lists work your AI sessions left open, crashes, uncommitted changes nobody touched for 3 days or more, and open pull requests, grouped by project. Three projects show at first; Show N more projects shows the rest. When there is nothing: “Nothing is left open right now.”
  • This week, Claude limit and To push sum up your week in three numbers.
  • Favorites shows the projects you starred. Star a project with the star next to its name.
  • Also in progress lists up to four other projects you worked on lately, with their last AI session. All projects opens the Projects page.

Keyboard

KeyWhat it does
EnterPresses the dark button on the big card
1–4Continues the last session of that row in Also in progress

The keys do nothing while you type in a field or a menu is open. A number key works only when that row has a session that can be reopened.

Tip: Nothing in progress yet? Home says so and offers New project and All projects. Once you work in a project, the big card takes you back to it.
