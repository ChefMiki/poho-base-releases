Get back to work

Your notes

Each project has a notes field that saves itself; your AI tools read it when you pick up the work.

Open a project, choose the Notes tab and type. There is no save button: PoHo saves your notes a moment after you stop typing, and says Saving…, then Saved.

What to write

Anything you want to find next time: where you stopped, what’s next, a command you keep forgetting. The field asks: “Where did you stop? What’s next?”

Where your notes show up

  • Welcome back on the project’s Map tab, and the next steps on Home, can point back to your note.
  • Pick up with … passes your notes to the AI tool, together with where the work stopped.
  • New Claude Code and Cursor chats get them in what PoHo tells the AI when a chat starts, when you have that turned on. See What your AI is told when a chat starts.
  • If you connected an Obsidian vault, the project’s note there includes them under “Your notes (from PoHo)”.

Beside the notes

At a glance shows when the project was last active, when you last opened it from PoHo, its last AI chat and its last commit.

Activity lists what happened in the project, day by day: found by PoHo, opened in the editor, a terminal opened, the dev server started or stopped, notes changed, an AI summary written. The same thing done several times in a row shows once, for example “Opened in the editor ×3”.

Safe by design

Your notes live in PoHo’s own memory on this computer, not in the project folder. Writing notes never changes your project’s files. Notes stay when PoHo looks at your folders again, and when a project is archived.

Tip: The filter on the Projects page searches your notes too, so a word you wrote in a note finds the project.
