Connect other apps

Obsidian

Keep your projects, AI sessions, collections and skills as linked notes in an Obsidian vault, and browse them in the graph view.

Connect an Obsidian vault and PoHo keeps a note for every project, collection and skill your AI used, and for your recent AI sessions, linked so the graph view shows how they connect. PoHo writes only into a PoHo folder in that vault and never touches your other notes.

Connect a vault

  1. Make or open a vault in Obsidian first. PoHo lists the vaults Obsidian knows.
  2. In PoHo, open Settings (Ctrl+,) and go to AI agents.
  3. Under Obsidian, pick the vault.
  4. Click Connect.

PoHo writes the notes right away. Open the vault in Obsidian and try the graph view.

What PoHo writes

Everything goes into <your vault>\PoHo:

  • PoHo Home: your projects by collection and your most used skills.
  • Projects: one note each, with what it is, where you stopped, what was left open, your notes, its AI sessions, the skills used there and the skills that fit.
  • Sessions: one note for each of your newest 300 AI sessions: what it did, what was left open, files touched and skills used.
  • Collections and Skills (skills your AI actually used, with the projects they were used in).

Your prompts are not copied, only titles and summaries. Keys, tokens and passwords are removed from every note.

Keeping it current

PoHo updates the notes after every scan. To update by hand, click Update now. Open in Obsidian opens the vault.

On a project page, More ways → Open its note in Obsidian jumps straight to that project’s note.

Your edits are safe

  • If you edit a note PoHo wrote, PoHo leaves it alone from then on and tells you how many it skipped.
  • PoHo never overwrites a note it didn’t write and never writes outside the PoHo folder.
  • PoHo never deletes a note. Notes of a project you removed stay.
  • PoHo doesn’t change Obsidian’s settings or its vault list.

Disconnect stops the updates; the notes stay in your vault.

Note: PoHo sends nothing over the internet for this. If you sync the vault, for example with Obsidian Sync or a cloud folder, the notes travel with it.
