The AI add-on (Ollama or a Claude key)

An optional AI that explains each project and session in plain words, running free on your computer through Ollama or with your own Claude API key.

PoHo works fully without AI. The AI add-on is optional: it writes what each project is about, what each AI session did, and where you stopped as a short, clear story. You choose a free AI on your own computer (Ollama) or your own Claude API key.

Turn it on

  1. Open Settings (Ctrl+,) and go to AI add-on.
  2. Choose one:
    • No AI: everything still works, built from your files and sessions.
    • Local AI: free, runs on this computer, works offline.
    • Claude API: your own API key, paid per use.

Local AI with Ollama

PoHo checks whether your computer can run a local model and suggests one that fits your graphics card. Then follow the steps:

  1. Install Ollama (click Get Ollama).
  2. Download the model. PoHo shows the progress; a model is a few gigabytes.
  3. Summarize your projects: click Summarize all. It runs in the background, one project at a time.

If Ollama is already installed, PoHo finds it and the models you have, and picks the best one.

Your own Claude key

  1. Choose Claude API.
  2. Paste your key into Anthropic API key and click Save.

The key is encrypted with Windows’ own protection when it is available. Remove deletes it from PoHo.

What it does

  • A summary on each project page: what it is, how it’s built and what to look at next. Click Summarize with AI or Regenerate.
  • A title, what was done and what is left for each AI session.
  • A clearer “where you stopped”.

Turn on Automatically summarize new and changed projects to keep them current. Summaries use only facts from your files and sessions.

What is sent where

  • Local AI: text goes to Ollama on this computer. Only downloading a model contacts ollama.com.
  • Claude API: PoHo sends Anthropic the README, CLAUDE.md and AGENTS.md (shortened), file names, text shown on the app’s screens, and what you asked your AI agents and their last replies (which can quote code). Never whole source files. Keys, tokens and passwords are removed first.
