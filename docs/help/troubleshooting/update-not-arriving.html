Troubleshooting

An update doesn’t arrive

How often PoHo looks for updates, how to check now, and why an update may not install.

PoHo looks for a new version a minute after it starts and then every 30 minutes. To check now, open Settings → Updates and click Check for updates.

Check now

  1. Open Settings and more (the gear in the top bar) → Settings.
  2. Go to Updates.
  3. Click Check for updates.

The line under your version tells you what happened:

  • “You have the newest version.”
  • “<version> is downloaded and checked. It installs when PoHo restarts.” Click Restart now, or it installs when you quit.
  • An error, such as “This release is not signed, so PoHo will not install it.”

When PoHo sits in the tray with nothing running, it can also install a ready update quietly by itself.

Why an update may not arrive

  • It is a beta. New versions go to beta users first. To get them too, turn on Get beta versions first in Settings → Updates. Without it, you get only stable versions.
  • You went back from that version. After Go back to version X, PoHo skips the version you left until a newer one comes out.
  • It is not signed, or it was changed on the way. PoHo installs only updates signed by its developer and refuses anything else.
  • The download was damaged. PoHo refuses it; check again later.
  • GitHub can’t be reached. Updates come from PoHo’s releases on GitHub. Without internet, PoHo can’t check.

Note: Before every update, PoHo copies your PoHo data. Your projects, notes and memory stay. If a new version gives you trouble, use Go back in Settings → Updates.

See also Updates and beta versions.
