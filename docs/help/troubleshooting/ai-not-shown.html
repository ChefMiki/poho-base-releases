Troubleshooting

PoHo doesn’t show my AI working

Why “AI working” or Live now may stay empty, and what to check.

PoHo shows your AI as working while its chat file keeps changing, and for 3 minutes after the last change. Check that PoHo may read your AI sessions, that the chat runs in a project PoHo knows, and that the briefing is connected.

How PoHo knows

  • The chat file. About every 10 seconds, PoHo checks the chat files of recent sessions. If one changed in the last 3 minutes, the AI counts as working. This works for Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode.
  • The briefing connection. When it is connected, Claude Code and Cursor also tell PoHo the moment they wait for you or finish. Claude Code’s finish ends “working” at once.

You see it on Home: the AI working chip on the project, and the Live now card.

What to check

  1. PoHo may read your AI sessions. Open Settings → Privacy & safety and make sure Read AI coding sessions is on.
  2. The chat runs in a project PoHo knows. A chat in a folder PoHo does not know is not shown. Add the folder: Add your project folders.
  3. The briefing is connected. Open Settings → AI agents and, under Briefing at the start of a session, click Connect for Claude Code or Cursor. This works only in the installed app.
  4. Start a new chat. Chats that were already open don’t pick up a new connection.

Things that are normal

  • It says finished while the AI is thinking. If the AI writes nothing for 3 minutes, for example during a long command, PoHo shows it as finished.
  • Cursor never shows “waiting for you”. Cursor does not report it.
  • Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode have no notifications. They show working and finished from their chat files only.
  • Short replies don’t notify. “Claude finished” is sent only after a task of 2 minutes or more.
  • After a restart, Live now starts empty. PoHo keeps live states in memory only.

See also What your AI is told when a chat starts.
