Welcome

Map of the app

The screens of PoHo Base, what is on each, and where PoHo’s information comes from.

PoHo has three pages, Home, Projects and History, plus a page for each project and one Settings window. The top bar takes you to all of them.

Top bar

  • Home, Projects, History: the three pages.
  • Jump to a project… (Ctrl P): search for a project or for something you asked an AI. Alt+Space opens the same search from any app.
  • New project: start a new project from a template.
  • ⚙ (Settings and more): Settings (Ctrl ,), AI add-on, Look for new projects, this computer’s name, Welcome screen, Help center and Report a bug.
  • The back arrow (Alt+Left) returns to the page you came from.

Home

The big card shows the project you were last in, where you stopped and what comes next, with one button to get back in. Below it:

  • Discover new skills for a project;
  • This week, Claude limit and To push (commits not on GitHub yet);
  • Favorites and Also in progress.

On the side: Live now (AI tools working or waiting), Needs you (loose ends and pull requests) and Your tools.

Projects

Tabs: All, Favorites and Skills (your skills library, with Discover to find new ones). Running and Archived appear when there is something in them. Next to the tabs are your collections, New collection and Sort automatically. Add folder adds more project folders.

A project’s page

The top shows the project, its git state and the main button (Continue in …, Pick up with … or Open in), plus More ways. Tabs:

  • Map: the project in the centre with its AI tools, code, dev server and services around it.
  • Sessions: every AI chat in this project.
  • Notes: your own notes and recent activity.
  • Setup: run command, files for AI agents, skills, picture and snapshots.

History

This week and Last week, Days with work, This month by project, Skills and tools you use, Is your code backed up? and AI use by month.

Settings

Pages: General, Projects & folders, AI add-on, AI agents, Editor & terminal, Tools, Notifications, Privacy & safety, Updates and Account & sync.

Where the information comes from

  • Your folders: names, READMEs, project files, in the folders you added.
  • Your AI tools’ chat history: the sessions Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, OpenCode and Antigravity save on this computer.
  • Git: branch, commits and uncommitted changes.
  • The optional AI add-on: summaries in plain words, only if you turn it on.

All of it stays on your computer, except the short excerpts the AI add-on sends to Claude when you use your own Claude key. A local AI (Ollama) sends nothing.
