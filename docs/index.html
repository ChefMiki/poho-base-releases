It finds every project.

Point PoHo at a folder, or let it scan your computer. It recognizes Git repos, apps and sites, works out the stack, and shows each one with a picture of what it looks like: its website, its open window or its icon.

2

It remembers every session.

Your Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and OpenCode chats are read from your computer and kept, even after the agent deletes them. What you asked, which files changed, how long it took, which skills you used.

3

It takes you back.

Today opens with the project you were in, where you stopped and the next step. Press Enter and the exact conversation reopens in your editor. Your agent even gets a short briefing when it starts.
